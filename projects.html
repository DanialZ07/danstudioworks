SRAI Qhalif Integrated System

Client: SRAI Qhalif Academy Timeline: 4 Weeks Stack: PHP / MySQL / Tailwind / REST API

The Challenge

SRAI Qhalif was managing monthly school fees (yuran) and student attendance via scattered physical receipt books and manual spreadsheets. Parents faced long queues during registration, and administrators lost over 30 hours each month manually matching bank statements.

The Engineering Solution

DanStudio Works engineered a bespoke cloud portal with automated FPX payment gateway integration, instant digital WhatsApp receipt dispatch, and an intuitive administrative backend for class teachers to log daily attendance in seconds.

Measurable Impact

  • 1,248 active students smoothly onboarded into digital records.
  • 99.4% timely fee collection rate achieved within first 60 days.
  • Zero lost receipts with automated PDF generation stored on secure cloud.
